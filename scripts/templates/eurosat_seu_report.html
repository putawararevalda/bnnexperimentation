01

Model variant

The four architectural variants: base, spike-suppressing SmartPool, dropout (p = 0.5), and weight decay (λ = 1e-4). Each row averages over all activations and priors in the slice.

The variant spread is far narrower than the run-to-run noise floor documented in docs/analysis/seu_metric_noise_floor.md — read the ordering as indistinguishable, not as a ranking.

02

Prior distribution

Gaussian and Laplace priors expose a location and a scale parameter; the uniform prior exposes a low bound and a width. Both parameterisations are injected.

03

Centre vs. spread parameters

Which half of the variational parameter is hit. locs/lows are centre parameters; scales/widths are spread parameters. A flip that drives a spread parameter negative is undefined and is excluded from every statistic on this page.

04

Activation function

Seven activations, including the two weighted-Gaussian variants (WG, RWG) whose bounded response is the reason they were introduced.

05

Injection site

Where in the network the upset lands — by layer, and by layer × parameter kind.

06

Bit position

IEEE-754 FP32 layout: bit 0 is the sign, bits 1–8 the exponent, bits 9–31 the mantissa. Bit 1 is the exponent MSB.

Bit 0 has half the sample count of the other positions: a sign flip on a spread parameter is always degenerate, so those rows are excluded.

07

Original bit value

Whether the upset raised a 0 to a 1 or cleared a 1 to a 0.

08

Prior scale

Cross-slice comparison — this table always pools every variant, prior and activation at each value of b, and does not respond to the selector above.

How to read these numbers

AAAD = mean |acc_after − acc_before|  ·  ASD = mean softmax difference  ·  ARIn = √((AAAD² + ASD²) / 2)
  1. ARIn is computed from the aggregated means, matching the paper, not averaged row-wise. The two differ; row-wise ARIn runs higher.
  2. b = 1.0 is the primary slice. It is the grid that matches the paper's single-scale BNN sweep, and recomputing ShipsNet fold-1 at b = 1.0 reproduces the paper's Table 3 BNN row exactly. The other slices are a sensitivity check.
  3. 3,024 injections are degenerate — a bit flip that makes a scale or width negative has no defined posterior. They are excluded from all means and counted separately, never silently dropped.
  4. One run per config, unseeded MC-10 inference. Run-to-run variance is unquantified; treat aggregate differences below ≈0.005 ARIn as noise.
  5. No deterministic-CNN baseline exists for EuroSAT. Every BNN-vs-DNN claim in the paper is untestable on this dataset — nothing here confirms or refutes it.
  6. Initial accuracy is the best-checkpoint MC-10 accuracy read from the SEU CSVs, not the last-epoch test accuracy in the training logs.
